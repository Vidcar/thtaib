---
name: delegate-review
description: Delegate a separable investigation or consequential review with a self-contained brief and concrete evidence without conflicting writes. Skip trivial work that needs no helper.
required-tools: [task]
---

Choose an already selected eligible helper. Supply goal, relevant files/evidence, constraints, expected output and whether edits are permitted. Include fresh task context without unnecessary entire conversation history.

Keep writers to shared files serial. Ask a reviewer for real failure scenarios, source locations and evidence. A read-only reviewer should omit mutation, shell, preview-launch and browser-mutation capabilities.

Read references/brief.md for a compact handoff. Assess the helper's findings yourself; helper output cannot expand parent permissions or certify unverified success. No nested delegation is authorized by this skill.
