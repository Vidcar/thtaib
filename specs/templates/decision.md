# ADR-NNNN: REPLACE_WITH_DECISION_TITLE

**Status:** draft | accepted | superseded. **Approval:** reviewer, reference, date (recorded in the catalogue; never invented). **Supersedes:** none or a record.

## Context

The problem, the current specification and evidence, and what the implementation does today. Two to four sentences.

## Decision

The ownership, dependency, contract, storage, permission or lifecycle change, precisely. Affected requirement IDs and the edits to their homes.

## Alternatives considered

Only those actually considered, each with the reason it lost.

## Consequences

What changes, what could break, migration and rollback, unresolved risk.

## Verification

The executable checks and live evidence required before the implementation counts as complete.

When a record is superseded, keep its original text and add a status banner naming the replacement; do not rewrite an old decision to look as if it always said the new thing.
